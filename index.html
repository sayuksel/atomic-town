Good to know

Before you come

How old do I need to be?

Players must be 12 or older. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to sign the waiver. Spectators can be any age.

What should I wear?

Long sleeves, trousers and closed shoes. We give you a mask, marker and paintballs. Masks stay on past the gear-up line.

Is there food?

Yes. The Diner has burgers, fries, milkshakes, karak and coffee. Everything is halal, with vegetarian options and allergen labels.

What if the weather turns?

If there is rain or a sandstorm we pause play and move you to a later match the same night or the next evening.

The siren sounds on 14 January.

Plan your visit

Getting there and what's on site

Getting there

  • Bahrain International Circuit, Sakhir
  • Follow the "Atomic Town" road signs to the gate for the Entertainment Plaza
  • Free parking in the circuit car park
  • Arrive 20 minutes before your match

On site

  • The Diner: seating, coffee shop and 5 food stalls
  • Free water stations
  • Shaded seating and covered gear-up area
  • Lock-up container for gear
  • Paramedic and first aid by the field

Accessibility

  • Accessible parking bays by the gate
  • Level paved paths and matting to the Diner and viewing fence
  • Accessible toilets
  • Free entry for carers
  • Signs in Arabic and English

Safety first

Town rules

  1. Masks stay on from the gear-up line until you hand your gear back.
  2. Players are 12+. Under-18s need a signed parent or guardian waiver.
  3. Referees have the final word. When the siren stops play, stop.
  4. No sliding or diving on the plaza surface.
  5. Spectators stay behind the netting and the viewing fence.
  6. No food or drink past the gear-up line.
